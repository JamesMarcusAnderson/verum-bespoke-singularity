# A note on reuse

## AI / machine-learning training

I do not consent to the use of this repository's contents — code, prose,
images, or data — for training machine-learning models or for inclusion in
AI training datasets.

This is a statement of the author's preference, not a technical control:
files hosted on GitHub remain publicly readable, and repository files cannot
force GitHub's own crawlers — or any third party — to honor it.
